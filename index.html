About RUNR

RUNR is Zambia's premier on-demand delivery ecosystem. Designed to bridge the gap between everyday needs and fast, reliable service.

Built with the vision of empowering local communities, RUNR connects Hirers (people who need things done) with RUNRs (dedicated drivers and individuals ready to run errands).

Founded by Wambulawae Daniel, we are committed to safety, speed, and trust. Whether it's groceries from Shoprite or documents to Arcades Mall, RUNR is always ready to move.
